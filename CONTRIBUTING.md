# Contributing

Corrections and improvements to the plan are welcome as issues and pull requests, under the same CC BY 4.0 license.

- Edit `business-plan.md`, then run `python scripts/build_plan.py` to refresh `business-plan.html`.
- Keep the plan company-neutral: use "the Company" for the reader's business and mark examples as examples.
- Do not add claims of guaranteed income or legal conclusions. Cite a source or say it is an assumption.
- Do not include customer data, credentials, or private inventories.
